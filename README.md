# Simple Robotic Dog Design

## Project Overview
In this project, I designed a simple robotic dog with four legs. The purpose of this project is to understand the basic mechanical design of a quadruped robot and learn how the robot can stand and move.

## Body and Frame
The robot has a main body, a head, and four legs. The body is designed to be simple and lightweight with enough space for the electronic components.

## Leg Design
Each leg is made of two parts connected by joints. This design allows the robot to perform simple walking movements while keeping its balance.

## Degrees of Freedom (DOF)
Each leg has two degrees of freedom, so the robot has a total of 8 degrees of freedom.

## Servo Motors
Servo motors are used because they are easy to control and suitable for moving the robot's joints.

## Torque Calculation
The torque can be calculated using the equation:

Torque = Force × Distance

The selected servo motor should provide enough torque to move the legs and support the robot's weight.

## Stability
The robot is designed with four legs to improve stability. The center of gravity is located near the middle of the body to help the robot balance while standing and walking.

## Assembly
The robot parts can be assembled using screws and servo motor brackets. The battery and controller can be placed inside the body.

## Expected Challenges
- Servo motors may become overloaded.
- The robot may lose balance during walking.
- Battery life may be limited.
- The joints may wear out after long use.

## Conclusion
This project helped me understand the basic mechanical design of a quadruped robot. In the future, the design can be improved by adding sensors and better control algorithms.
